# Phase 4: rolling-horizon re-planning

Generated by `validation/rolling.py`; do not edit by hand.

## Setup

48 actual years drawn week by week from the utilization model (seed 20261121, stream 4). Re-plan every 4 weeks; tasks decided at an epoch are committed until the next. Re-planning runs the phase 3 GA (40 x 50, warm-started with the current plan) on one scenario at the design rate, or on 30 scenarios sampled conditionally on the observed regimes and demand factor. Realised cost = planned tasks and visits + forced tasks + expected failures given the realised ages.
